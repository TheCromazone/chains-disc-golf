Inspected only `v1-board.png` at original resolution. Labels are hidden, but the differing scenes and rendering make this comparison identifiable rather than truly blind.

| Pair | Winner for cohesive leafy crown volume | Exactly one biggest gap in the weaker option |
|---|---|---|
| A / B | **A** | **B lacks convincing shaded crown interiors.** Its foreground broadleaf crown reads as bright, angular lumps pressed together; the overlaps do not establish much depth inside the foliage. |
| C / D | **D** | **C lacks convincing shaded crown interiors.** Its large foreground crown has a jagged outline, but the similarly lit lobes flatten into a clump instead of a dense, continuous leafy mass. |
| E / F | **E** | **F lacks convincing shaded crown interiors.** From above, the broadleaf trees expose shallow, faceted puffs with little dark overlap to make the crowns feel thick. |

The decisive gap is within the canopy in all three pairs. Irregular outlines alone do not compensate for weak internal depth.
