## Visual acceptance blockers

- **Character A / face B:** the shoulders resemble separate red balls, gloves resemble white blobs, and legs read as thin rods. Redesign the shoulder-to-sleeve join and hand silhouette; give the feet and limbs cleaner, deliberate proportions. Simplicity needs controlled shapes.
- **World A / HUD B:** the destination is hard to read, terrain is largely undifferentiated, and the interface occupies too much of the phone. A recognizable basket/fairway focal point and substantially more unobstructed course space are required.
- **Feedback A:** the celebrant is cut off at the left edge, the outcome is small, and a large empty grass area remains while inactive controls still clutter the frame. Reframe the whole character, enlarge the result, and suppress the shot controls during the celebration. This wins its pair but is not yet loud or polished enough.

The strongest finished direction is the mobile menu treatment. The largest remaining art gap is the transition from clean glass menus into a convincingly lit, composed playing world.
