    How to play

    Aim — drag anywhere on the course view. Drag up/down to change the launch angle. 🎯 snaps to the basket, 🗺️ shows the hole from above.
    Disc — Driver flies farthest but needs speed; Putter is straight and short. Flight numbers are Speed | Glide | Turn | Fade.
    Throw — pick a throw type, then swipe in the bottom pad in that throw's direction: Backhand →, Forehand ←, Tomahawk ↓, Scoober ↖, Putt ↑. Longer swipe = more power (watch the bar). Swipe slightly down for hyzer (more fade), up for anhyzer.
    Flight — a right-hand backhand fades left at the end; forehand fades right. Throw a disc faster than it's rated for and it will turn over.

    Rules

    Lowest total throws wins. Each hole starts on the tee pad; you play your next throw from where the disc came to rest. Farthest from the basket throws first; the best score on the previous hole tees off first. Holed when the disc rests in the basket or chains. Water and course boundaries are out of bounds: +1 throw, play from where it went out. Circle 1 is 10 m from the basket. Score names: Ace, Eagle, Birdie, Par, Bogey…
